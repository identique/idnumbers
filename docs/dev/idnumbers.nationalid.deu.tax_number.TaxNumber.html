class TaxNumber:

View In Hierarchy

Germany tax number, Steuernummer, St.-Nr., in the 13-digit ELSTER format

The tax offices of the federal states allocate the tax number. The ELSTER format is FFFF0BBBUUUUP, and FFFF0BBBBUUUP in North Rhine-Westphalia (display format FFFF0BBB{BU}UUUC covers both): the 4-digit federal tax office number (Bundesfinanzamtsnummer), whose leading digits are the state number, a 0, the district number (Bezirksnummer), the distinguishing number (Unterscheidungsnummer) and the check digit.

The rules follow chapters 3 to 7 of the ELSTER document "Prüfung der Steuer- und Steueridentifikationsnummer" (Bayerisches Landesamt für Steuern): https://download.elster.de/download/schnittstellen/Pruefung_der_Steuer_und_Steueridentifikatsnummer.pdf

  • The state number is one of the 16 federal states, and the 5th digit is 0.
  • The district number is not 000, 998 or 999 (0000, 0998 or 0999 in North Rhine-Westphalia), and it is at least 100 in the states of the Bavarian programming group (Bavaria, Brandenburg, Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania, Saarland, Saxony, Saxony-Anhalt and Thuringia).
  • In North Rhine-Westphalia, the distinguishing number and the check digit together are greater than 0009.
  • The check digit follows the method of the state: the 2er-Verfahren in Baden-Württemberg, Hesse and Schleswig-Holstein, the modified 11er-Verfahren in Rhineland-Palatinate, and the 11er-Verfahren with state-specific weights elsewhere. Berlin uses the weights Berlin-A or Berlin-B by tax office and district, as listed in section 7.2; a Berlin tax office missing from that table is rejected, because its method is unknown. Numbers whose check digit would have two digits are never allocated.

Only the ELSTER format is accepted. The format printed on tax notices (for example 198/815/08152) omits part of the federal tax office number, so it can't be validated reliably. The tax office itself is not checked against the list of offices, which ELSTER publishes separately and changes over time.
