class IcelandicID:

View In Hierarchy

Iceland Icelandic identification number, kennitala https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Icelandic_identification_number

The number has 10 digits, DDMMYY-SSKC: the date of birth, two serial digits, a ninth digit and the century digit. Þjóðskrá (Registers Iceland) says the last digit is 8, 9 or 0 and gives the century of birth, so C is 8 for 1800-1899, 9 for 1900-1999 and 0 for 2000-2099.

The ninth digit is not checked. Since 18 February 2026, Þjóðskrá issues every kennitala, both those of individuals registered in Þjóðskrá and system kennitölur, without a calculated check digit (vartala), and it asks systems to remove the check-digit test. The number carries no issue date, so a kennitala issued before that date, whose ninth digit is a mod-11 check digit, cannot be told apart from a new one. METADATA.checksum is False and there is no checksum() method. Þjóðskrá's six synthetic test kennitölur without a check digit, such as 281191-3049, validate.

validate() checks the 10 ASCII digits (with an optional hyphen after the date), the century digit and that the date of birth is a real calendar date. Kennitölur of legal entities (day of registration + 40) are not supported.

Sources:

  • Þjóðskrá, Um kennitölur (layout, century digit, the pre-2026 check digit and the 2026 change): https://www.skra.is/folk/eg-i-thjodskra/um-kennitolur/
  • Þjóðskrá, Allar kennitölur án vartölu (14 May 2025): https://www.skra.is/um-okkur/frettir/frett/2025/05/14/Kennitolur-an-vartolu/
  • Þjóðskrá, Gervigögn Þjóðskrár (test kennitölur without a check digit): https://www.skra.is/gogn/gervigogn-thjodskrar/
